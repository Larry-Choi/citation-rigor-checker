Compare only fields present on both sides; a match establishes agreement within this packet, not independent authentication. Missing title words or unexplained year/author discrepancies warrant metadata_review. A title-only or author-only discrepancy is metadata_review; identity_conflict means the assigned candidate identifies a different work with conflicting title and authors. These are task label definitions, not a claim about universal bibliographic policy.
If candidate_record_id is supplied, compare against that candidate, preserving wrong assignments. If it is null, inspect all available packet records for an unambiguous match. Use unresolved if no relevant usable record is present; do not rely on memory or external sources.
Semantic labels: supported = the supplied passage supports the whole claim; partially_supported = at least one substantive component is supported and another exceeds the passage; contradicted = direct incompatible evidence; not_supported = readable relevant passage lacks support for the attributed proposition; unresolved = access/identity/OCR prevents comparison; not_assessed = no assessable claim supplied or outside scope. These judgments cover supplied passages only.
